About Gift

Aloha, I'm Gift.

A neighbor on Oʻahu who happens to be really good with numbers — and who never wants another homeowner to face scary mail alone.

Why I do this

I've seen how confusing a foreclosure notice, a probate letter, or a tax lien can look to someone who's never had to read one before — and how that confusion alone can cost people time they didn't know they had. I started Gift Homeowner Support so homeowners here on Oʻahu would have someone to call who isn't trying to sell them anything, isn't on the clock, and isn't going anywhere once the conversation gets hard.

No pressure, no judgment — just someone in your corner while you figure out what's next.

A little about my background

Numbers and financial systems have been my world for over a decade. I hold a Ph.D. in Business Administration and a Master's in Financial Analysis, and I currently teach full-time at a university here on Oʻahu. Along the way I've also worked in public-sector finance and spent years advising organizations on financial strategy.

I share that background not to impress you, but so you know the information you get from me is grounded in real financial training — the same rigor I'd want if it were my own family reading that notice.

My commitment to you

100% free, always. I volunteer my time because I believe every homeowner deserves to understand their options — not because I profit from your situation. There's no obligation, no sales pitch, and no pressure to decide anything before you're ready.

I'm a homeowner advocate and educator — not a licensed attorney or HUD-approved housing counselor. I don't provide legal or tax advice, and when specialized help is needed, I connect you with trusted, verified professionals.
